# Mininet: fair compensation and a 200-year economic plan

**Status: proposed policy and conditional planning values; not adopted, funded, audited or an investment forecast.** Prepared 12 September 2026 against Mininet source `8d7dbe8512720ef2a61c4d2a94127fb8ddf66623`.

The offline review tool is `tools/sim/economics-planning/index.html`. The companion `economics-generations.md` extends issuance and wealth scenarios to 1,000 years. Figures in this document describe its separate 200-year and compensation defaults only; no hosted update is claimed.

## 1. Recommendation

Recognise the founder substantially, finitely and on evidence. Pay an active founder at the same rate as another person doing comparable work. Pay temporary councillors for defined duties and time, never for political outcomes. Protect the equal Human Share from all three groups. Future generations must inherit economic entry, functioning infrastructure and replaceable institutions, not a permanent bill payable to the founding generation.

Use three separate accounting categories:

1. **Human Share:** a perpetual equal issuance rule, not a payroll or founder fund.
2. **Earned work:** scoped wages, bounties and evidenced network services, with independent acceptance.
3. **Voluntary sponsor operating funds:** actual unrestricted resources belonging to consenting, replaceable contributors or sponsors for operations, accepted liabilities, audits and reserves. This is not a protocol-owned bank account, incorporated Mininet owner, legacy-currency issuer or custodial relationship. FD-18 and the Failure Book exclude those constructions. Quoted token value is not cash.

A founder's personal hardship matters morally, but a public award cannot be calibrated by unverifiable suffering, a private valuation of the project's potential, or permanent ownership of future output. Document work, unreimbursed project costs and prior payments. Open the same process to other early contributors. Separate recognition of freely contributed work from genuine existing contractual debt; a new discretionary award cap cannot cancel wages or debts already owed.

## 3. Concrete pay framework

All amounts are gross reference euros, before personal tax. They are policy starting points, not asserted market quotes. Use an annually approved reference basket and review full pay bands at least every five years. Same role and accepted scope should use the same core band; any accessibility, local-cost or specialist allowance needs objective criteria and disclosed costs, never nationality-based political rights.

The default base is **€48,000 for a full-time year**. For dated context, CSO reports 2024 median annual earnings of €44,816 overall and €80,147 in information/communication. These figures cover employments active at least 50 weeks and are not a global living-wage standard or an independent security-audit quote. The proposal's base is a judgment, not a figure prescribed by those data. [CSO, published 29 July 2025](https://www.cso.ie/en/releasesandpublications/ep/p-eaads/earningsanalysisusingadministrativedatasources2024/annualearnings/).

| Role | Rule | Default annual | Monthly equivalent |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| General contributor | 1.0 × base, full time | €48,000 | €4,000 |
| Founder doing a lead role | 1.5 × base, full time | €72,000 | €6,000 |
| Another comparable lead | Same 1.5 × band | €72,000 | €6,000 |
| Temporary councillor | 1.2 × base × 0.25 FTE | €14,400 | €1,200 |
| External review procurement | 2.0 × base, total annual envelope | €96,000 | €8,000 budget equivalent |

The external review figure is a procurement budget, not a salary, quotation or assertion that it purchases a complete audit. Get independent written scopes and quotes. The default staffing model contains six general contributor FTEs, one founder/lead role, six council seats and that review envelope. The comparable senior rate illustrates fairness; it is not another funded headcount.

Propose six-month council terms, at most two consecutive terms and a one-term break. Composition and authority still follow the constitutional/domain rules. Pay for documented interface reviews, availability, handovers and accepted coordination work. No payment per favourable vote, token price rise or number of approvals. A councillor who also contributes cannot bill the same hours or deliverable twice. Publish conflicts and use non-conflicted reviewers; a paid group cannot approve its own pay rise. Time, review burden and actual duties need measurement before terms are contracted.

Work offers should state asset, amount, acceptance criteria, privacy mode, split awards, dispute rules and funding before work begins. Technical acceptance, claim control, payment destination and political authority remain separate. No public legal-name requirement should be introduced merely to administer compensation. [Bounty lifecycle](https://github.com/mininet-labs/Mininet/blob/8d7dbe8512720ef2a61c4d2a94127fb8ddf66623/docs/governance/29_ANONYMOUS_BOUNTY_LIFECYCLE.md), [Compensation settlement](https://github.com/mininet-labs/Mininet/blob/8d7dbe8512720ef2a61c4d2a94127fb8ddf66623/docs/governance/30_COMPENSATION_PRIVACY_AND_SETTLEMENT.md).

## 4. Founder retrospective recognition

**The user's actual work history and expenses have not been supplied.** The following is an illustration to replace with dated evidence:

- Three years of unpaid work at an average 0.75 FTE.
- Comparable lead rate €72,000/year: 3 × 0.75 × €72,000 = **€162,000** labour recognition.
- €15,000 of documented, unreimbursed project expenses; this amount is assumed, not established.
- Zero prior labour payments assumed. Deduct actual earlier payment and exclude duplicate claims.
- Proposed new-award labour cap: four base-rate years (€192,000). Expenses require receipts; they are not subject to a fictitious salary multiplier.

**Illustrative finite award: €177,000**, with a five-year payment target of **€35,400/year**, including expenses. Apply the same recognition scheme to other qualifying early contributors. On a proposed 1,800-hour planning year the base corresponds to €26.67/hour and the lead band €40/hour; hours are evidence for valuation, not an incentive to log meaningless activity.

Assuming a 20% on-cost allowance on the labour component, the full funding requirement is **€209,400**. Do not promise the award until an independent review accepts it and that total can be protected separately from the operating reserve. Recipient timing and legal treatment need an actual agreement; the model uses a five-year funding schedule, not a legal conclusion about grant vesting or employment status.

Proposed annual disbursement is the lesser of the scheduled installment and 10% of the annual earned-work envelope. With the default €542,400 envelope, the cap does not restrict the €35,400 installment. Once offered, the agreed schedule and recipient rights cannot be retrospectively reduced by a later change in budget. In other scenarios, determine the entire schedule before the agreement is accepted; do not promise five years if the annual cap requires longer.

The award must **not** require the founder to keep working, retain office, secure a token listing, reach a price target or keep political control. Do not condition payment for already accepted past work on a future auditor's availability. Escrow is not a general treasury emergency fund. Existing rights of an estate or beneficiary depend on the agreement and applicable law; the proposal gives descendants no founder seat, salary or new Human Share stream. Vested private property is a separate matter from hereditary governance.

While working full time, the default founder receives €72,000 current-role compensation plus the €35,400 scheduled past-work payment, or **€107,400/year for the illustrated five-year period**. This is 1.5 times the general rate for present work, and 2.2375 times including the finite retrospective installment. After the award is paid, only active-role pay remains. Someone doing the same lead work receives the same rate; authority ends independently of payment.

Approaching donors for a founder-support grant is compatible with ordinary voluntary funding; it must not purchase political influence.

## 6. What normal participants receive — and what they do not

Default monetary inputs: **1 billion circulating MINI, 100,000 eligible real humans, €0.10 per MINI**, with no falsely accepted Sybils. These are normalisation/scenario inputs, not approved genesis or a live quote.

At the opening annualised run-rate:

- Human pool: 20 million MINI/year.
- Each eligible human: **200 MINI/year**, notionally **€20/year or €1.67/month** at the assumed price.
- Founder, councillor and worker receive the same formula if eligible. Work compensation is additional, not a larger Human Share.
- Accrual vests over 365 days. Starting with no prior grants, the monthly model's first year accrues about 201.36 MINI/person and releases about 91.99 MINI/person into circulation. The latter is not automatically sold for euros.

**This is not a living-income guarantee.** For a target €50/month, the identity `quoted circulating value = annual target × eligible population / 0.02` implies €30,000 of quoted circulating value per eligible human. At 100,000 people, that is €3 billion; at ten billion people, **€300 trillion**. A price label does not supply liquidity or redeemability. Changing token denomination changes no purchasing power.

Proposed illustrative service-envelope split: 50% infrastructure, 30% builders, 10% independent assurance, 10% public-commons work. These are reviewable work budgets; they cannot become hereditary rights or bypass evidence. A councillor's integration work belongs to a scoped work budget, not the Human Share. This split is not implemented policy.

At full opening service capacity, 7.5 million MINI/year are available before actual allocation. With 1,000 equally qualified providers and a 50% infrastructure allocation, each gets 3,750 MINI/year: €375 quoted gross at €0.10. Against assumed annual costs of €120, that is €255 before Human Share and personal tax. If MINI falls 80% while costs do not, gross falls to €75 and the provider loses €45. Actual concavity, cost diversity, utilisation, delayed evidence and sellable liquidity still need measurement. The existing local reward stub does not establish a production earning opportunity.

The default cash staffing budget does not additionally book these tokens as treasury revenue. If actual offers use both cash and MINI, precommit how token value reduces the cash obligation and prevent duplicate payment for the same accepted unit. Nobody can promise a fixed euro amount from thin-market MINI without a real funding counterparty bearing the difference.

## 7. Two hundred years: bounded money is necessary, not sufficient

The planner tracks monthly issuance and circulating/locked supply. Human Share is equally divided across each epoch's eligible count, including any fake roots deliberately introduced as an adversarial assumption. New grants start vesting at epoch end. Service grants are immediately liquid in this envelope model; actual evidence-specific delays are omitted. No burn, confiscation or hidden founder mint occurs.

The input population is the effective active eligible count. Births, deaths, individual grace periods and personal migration are not separately simulated. Population reaches a selected target over 50 years and then remains constant. Available valuation scenarios are fixed total quoted value, fixed quoted value per participant and fixed token price. These are conditional accounting assumptions, not forecasts. Fixed token price implicitly requires aggregate quoted value to grow with supply; it is not a free return assumption. All euro comparisons are in constant reference purchasing-power units.

A passive holder who never reacquires tokens loses percentage share as others receive new issuance. That does not prove equal wealth, adequate wages or freedom from bribery. The model separately allows a tracked holder to capture a share of service issuance. It does not simulate trading all new Human Share, off-network coercion, a full wealth distribution or inheritance. No Gini or governance-capture success claim is fabricated.

**Annual cap ambiguity worth resolving:** the inspected issuance and ledger code use current epoch-opening circulating supply for annualised caps. Per-epoch ceilings do not alone define a cumulative year-opening budget. The planner's protocol-like monthly scenario reaches a maximum of about 3.04119% gross issuance against a year's opening circulating base. Its optional proposed annual guard holds that definition at 3%, protects the monthly Human Share first and reduces optional service/contribution room. This is a surfaced policy/implementation question, not an adopted monetary change or a claimed production exploit. Reproduce it against canonical Rust tests, specify policy-time/year boundaries and agree the intended base before amendment.

The existing Rust cohort harness also does not model market prices, treasury assets, oracle failures or complete reward allocation. Neither it nor this planning model closes independent mechanism-design review. [Simulation gate](https://github.com/mininet-labs/Mininet/blob/8d7dbe8512720ef2a61c4d2a94127fb8ddf66623/docs/gates/economic-simulation-spec.md), [Ledger](https://github.com/mininet-labs/Mininet/blob/8d7dbe8512720ef2a61c4d2a94127fb8ddf66623/crates/mini-economy/src/ledger.rs).

A future reserve with zero real return cannot be presumed to fund infinite expenditure. Keep useful recurring services, voluntary funders, independent archives, rotating custodians and a controllable operating footprint. Reassess economic parameters at stated intervals while preserving vested property and constitutional rights. The future child inherits the same current Human Share formula, not a claim to the founding generation's grant.

## 8. Shocks and multiplanetary operation

At the D-0073 50/40/10 bridge split, an 80% XRP loss removes 40% of the bridge reserve; an 80% XMR loss removes 32%; simultaneous 80% losses remove **72%**. A €1 million bridge reserve then retains €280,000 if the contingency is unaffected. These are mechanical shocks, not probabilities. Correlated custody, issuer, gateway and payroll-asset failures require separate testing. Crypto valuations and liquidity can change sharply; do not count them as guaranteed operational funding. [ECB financial-stability analysis, May 2025](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/financial-stability-publications/fsr/special/html/ecb.fsrart202505_01~62255f2625.en.html).

For Mars, NASA describes one-way delays up to roughly 21–23 minutes and communications blackouts lasting weeks. [NASA communications white paper](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/mars-communications-disruption-and-delay.pdf).

An illustrative local autonomy buffer is:

`(21 outage days + 14 reconciliation/margin days) × €1,000 daily essential resources / (1 − 20% usability haircut) = €43,750 equivalent pre-positioned resources`.

This must mean real locally usable supplies or already-funded services. A bag of global MINI that cannot settle through a partition is not equivalent to food, power or immediately available local credit. The euro is only today's reference unit. Avoid planetary wage multipliers that buy authority; demonstrate real local logistics costs separately.

Local messaging, work records, coordination and provisioning can continue. Global treasury execution, canonical ownership changes and monetary rule changes cannot pretend a local decision is globally final. Do not reuse older speculative 'local settlement zones' wording as permission for a second final history of the same MINI. Current D-0513 preserves pending claims and explicit later reconciliation. Presence during long partitions and periods exceeding grace need an accessibility design and exact rules; this calculator does not grant unlimited retroactive Human Share. [DTN constraints and current update](https://github.com/mininet-labs/Mininet/blob/8d7dbe8512720ef2a61c4d2a94127fb8ddf66623/docs/gates/dtn-design-constraints.md).

## 11. Validation and limits

The included deterministic checks exercise 60 combinations across 200 years: five population targets (including decline and 1/10/20 billion), three service-utilisation levels, two contribution-utilisation levels and two assumed Sybil populations. They check supply conservation and the proposed annual cap, plus separate tests for vesting, zero funding, paid-award exhaustion, protected escrow during an inflow stop, bridge-reserve separation and the delay buffer.

Passing those checks means arithmetic behaves as specified. It is not an economic safety audit, market forecast, legal review, proof of personhood or a statement that all canonical stress gates passed. Floating-point values are suitable for scenario visualisation, not consensus or disbursement; canonical MINI code must keep checked integer arithmetic. Monthly timing is an approximation to a 365-day year. No live population, actual founder history, real treasury balance or executable token market was supplied.


## Scope clarification after purchasing-power review

The companion 1,000-year proposal targets stable purchasing power **per MINI**, not merely stable value per person. Its target-compatible issuance path is distinct from the current protected issuance rules and is not activated by this proposal. All reference-EUR operating balances in this document belong to voluntary sponsors; the accounting model creates no protocol-level bank account, employer, fiat custodian or authority over participants.
