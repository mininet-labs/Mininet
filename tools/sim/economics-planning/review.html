The UN series covers 2026–2100. Beyond 2100, these are stress scenarios with no assigned probability. “100% wealth” is a valuation premise—not ownership, backing, redemption, market demand or promised income.

Target: the same basket of goods per MINI

The target path holds one MINI’s buying-power index at 100 and calculates compatible net issuance. The existing-policy path applies the issuance envelope instead. If the two diverge, stable buying power requires a policy change or greater real demand—not a different label on the price.

What makes one MINI worth anything?

Reference price = attributed fraction × external wealth ÷ total issued MINI

The target issuance path is a counterfactual accounting condition, not an automatic price stabilizer. It does not enforce the protected Human Share floor, service or contribution rules. Zero wealth attribution makes the opening price and buying-power target undefined. The denominator includes locked grants because they are already issued economic interests. A circulating-supply quotation is also exported for comparison; applying it to all issued tokens would value MINI above the attributed wealth budget.

Wealth is a stock of net assets. GDP and wages are annual flows. Calling MINI a unit of account does not transfer assets to it. A wealth-backed claim would require enforceable rights and custody that this proposal does not create. A circulating currency instead needs willing holders, productive exchange and liquidity. Do not add token capitalization to the same underlying assets and call that new wealth.

Default wealth is an illustrative USD 500 trillion in constant reference purchasing power, not a measured 2026 total or a price target. The price can be zero. Higher population increases aggregate wealth only because this scenario explicitly holds wealth per person constant.

Finite recognition. Comparable pay for comparable work.

This is a separately funded voluntary sponsor operating budget in reference EUR. It is independent of the USD wealth premise above. The founder gets no privileged issuance, permanent percentage or bought authority.

Other assumptions: six full-time contributors, six quarter-time councillors, EUR 96,000 audit budget, EUR 120,000 operating costs, 20% wage on-costs, 18-month core reserve, EUR 15,000 unreimbursed expenses. Council proposal: six-month terms, at most two consecutive terms, then one term off. Every figure requires evidence, funding and independent review.

Limits, failure cases and source trail

Balances use exact integer micro-MINI, with unminted equal-allocation remainders. USD values are approximate floating-point calculations. Monthly epochs approximate policy time, not civil calendar months. No fees, burns, defaults, live exchange prices, demographic cohorts, death/claim succession or service-market equilibrium are simulated.

Partial participation and Sybil inputs are independent of wealth attribution. A few early participants claiming all human wealth is an extreme stress case, not a fair launch policy. Identity roots are not proven unique humans. Multiplanetary population scenarios do not solve transport delays, local resource supply or global finality.
